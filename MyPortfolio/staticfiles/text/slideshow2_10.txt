Ladakh, India*****
Riding through the rugged terrain of Ladakh on a motorbike is an adventure like no other, and the journey to **Pangong Lake** is its crown jewel. The ride takes you across dramatic high‑altitude passes, winding mountain roads, and remote valleys before opening up to the surreal beauty of Pangong Tso — a shimmering lake that famously changes colors from deep blue to turquoise and even golden hues.
