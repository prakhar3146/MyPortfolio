Komodo Island, Indonesia*****
Komodo Island in Indonesia is world‑renowned as the natural habitat of the mighty **Komodo dragon**, the largest living lizard on Earth. These prehistoric‑looking creatures can grow up to three meters long and are found only in Komodo National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage site. The island itself is stunning, with rugged hills, pink‑sand beaches, and vibrant coral reefs that make it a paradise for divers and trekkers alike.
